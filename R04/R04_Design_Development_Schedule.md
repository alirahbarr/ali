# R04 — Restaurant architecture and material development schedule

348 Front Street restaurant · Revision R04 · 16 October 2026 (Toronto date supplied for this issue)

**Approved visual basis:** The owner has approved the compact direction shown in `07_Renderings/R03_V02_Compact_Form_Study.png`. This is a definitive visual-direction decision; it does not establish a site-fitted layout, dimensions, construction assembly, selected product, statutory approval or procurement release. The image itself is a form/material study, not a site layout.

**R04 objective:** Develop that selected composition into a coherent, maintainable restaurant: a compact warm-brick front portal and curved cashier, opaque kitchen concealment, an enclosed rear/left kitchen, a clear independent right guest corridor, concrete-look floors and a quiet upstairs setting with warm wood furniture and a Persian rug. R02 material options and R03 reference translation remain background records; the selected compact V02 form now governs visual development where earlier alternatives differ.

## 2. Staff access, transaction and service strategy

**Develop kitchen-side staff entry first.** Test a concealed kitchen-side return/door into the cashier work zone so staff can replenish packaging and transfer orders without a counter gate or door swinging into the guest corridor. Its exact position and arrangement are not selected. The compact concept image does not show a buildable staff entrance; that omission must be resolved in plan before fixing the piers, backing or counter carcass.

Keep the right guest corridor independent and continuous to the stair and any approved facilities. Do not solve cashier access by inventing a door across it, using it as a packaging store or placing a permanent delivery queue there. Plot the open staff-door positions, occupied counter, waiting customers, delivery pickup, ground seating and guest movement together. The owner target remains approximately five or six ground-floor dining places, subject to approved fit and circulation; this schedule does not confirm capacity.

| Operation | Development basis | Check in the next plan / detail |
|---|---|---|
| Order/payment | A clear part of the curved counter serves payment without exposing the kitchen. Integrate an appropriate accessible transaction position into the approved form. | Counter/workstation accessibility, payment device reach, knee/approach requirements where applicable, glare, privacy, staff visibility and serviceability. Do not invent legal dimensions from the image. |
| Order dispatch | Transfer finished orders from the enclosed kitchen to staff behind the cashier via a screened internal route, with packaging and labelling kept orderly. | If a pass/opening is required, show its actual location, hygiene/temperature handling and any required fire/smoke closure/performance. If it compromises enclosure or concealment, use the screened staff-side route instead. An arbitrary open hatch is not assumed acceptable. |
| Pickup | Identify a distinct pickup position within the cashier composition or another fitted location, so collection does not occupy the payment point or guest corridor. | Peak takeout/delivery demand, number of orders, hot/cold holding, bag sizes and controlled visibility. Do not size shelves or purchase holding equipment until those inputs and fit are available. |
| Replenishment / storage | Bring packaging and supplies from the kitchen side into dedicated concealed cashier storage. | Do not use the finished canopy top or a required corridor for storage. Keep controls, shutoffs and service access reachable. |
| Dining service / returns | Test a route to the mezzanine that keeps carried food, dirty returns and guests as orderly as the actual layout permits. | Landing/door conflicts, stair safety, staff passing and clean/dirty segregation. No new service stair or guest-through-kitchen route is proposed. |
| Cashier entry / exit | Show a workable kitchen-side entry and a safe staff escape arrangement. | Door swing, equipment conflicts, closing/locking, required egress and applicable workplace/accessibility provisions. Do not make a staff member climb over a fixed counter or step onto the canopy. |

The owner-resolved refrigeration side remains **left when entering, sheet top on landscape A5**. Preserve the cooler/freezer functions and verify actual equipment/door/utility clearances separately. Their exact selected positions are not established here. Maintain the current required washroom/accessibility strategy; the hoped-for future ground-floor universal-washroom conversion is not available storage in this plan.

## 6. Improvements beyond the approved appearance

- The compact portal becomes a coordinated enclosure/cashier system with designed backing, staff entry and dispatch, rather than a freestanding attractive object with no operating route.
- A deliberately finished and maintainable non-walkable top resolves the mezzanine view while keeping the structure and kitchen assembly distinct.
- Ground, kitchen, stairs and mezzanine share a concrete appearance through **different appropriate assemblies**; floor level, stair consistency, moisture and load are resolved together.
- Integrated task/circulation light and tested absorption make the warm, hard-surfaced composition usable during service, cleaning and occupied dining.
- Finish IDs, product evidence and measured trade scope provide a path from the definitive visual approval to technically reviewable construction information without inventing fit, products, legal dimensions or a budget total.
